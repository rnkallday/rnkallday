About the Author

Rian Kochel was that kid you went to high school with whose locker always looked like a bomb went off in it. Since then, he has lived a thousand different lives—all of them as messy and multifarious as that locker he left behind.

This is his first book, but his personal essays have appeared in The Sun Magazine and HuffPost.

About the book

Surviving Queer

A Memoir

by Rian Kochel

A beautiful, blistering, and fiercely funny testament to surviving the closet, reclaiming your history, and breaking the cycles of family trauma.

Surviving Queer is an unforgettable, raw, and poetic memoir—a love letter to every lost, lonely queer kid who has been forced to hide from their truth in order to feel safe. It carries an urgent message of indelible hope—a plea for them to hold onto their truth, carry it forward, and use it as the foundation for their future.
