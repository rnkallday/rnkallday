How I Work With AI

A 6-step framework for authentic, human-driven AI collaboration.

1
The Strategy
Define Goal & Persona

Define the objective and the AI's role before writing a prompt. Set the "who" and the "what."

2
The Input
Provide Detailed Context & Constraints

Supply background, audience, and situation. Explicitly state what not to do.

3
The Blueprint
Use Examples / Few-Shot Prompting

Show 2–3 high-quality examples of the desired output style, format, or tone.

4
The Layout
Structure the Output Format

Specify the format to make results immediately usable. Markdown, lists, tables, scripts, etc.

5
Human-in-the-Loop
Review & Refine

Iterate. Edit, request alternatives, use chain-of-thought prompting. AI is a collaborator, not a shortcut.

6
The Safety Check
Verify Ethics, Accuracy & Brand Alignment

Audit for hallucinations, bias, and brand consistency before publishing.
